PYPlym / WHY IT MATTERS 02

The case for learning to use AI well

AI can help us perform.
But are we learning?

A study of high-school maths shows why the way we use AI matters.

01 / During practice

AI at hand.
Better scores.

Students using unrestricted ChatGPT performed better on practice problems.

→

02 / Exam without AI

−17%exam scores

Those students then scored lower than students who had practised without AI.

Design for thinking,
not just answers.

A structured AI tutor that gave hints, using teacher-written solutions and common mistakes, avoided this harm.

Study context: high-school maths in Turkey. 17% is a relative drop. The tutor did not outperform the no-AI group; a simple tutor prompt is a different intervention.

SOURCE: BASTANI ET AL. · PNAS (2025)
